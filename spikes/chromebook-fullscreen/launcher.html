Spike: try these, then copy the results

  1. Go full screen (below, or the full-screen key). Open a service, start a video.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+H: does the launcher come back, still full screen?
  3. Press Alt+Shift+J, or choose the same service again: does it come back where you left it?
  4. Put the video in the site's own full screen (its player button, or f), then press Alt+Shift+H again.
  5. Repeat with two or three of your services, and try a search for a show.
