# The household viewer

<!-- docs/product/personas/viewer.md. Anyone who watches the family-room TV and operates it with
the remote: the other adult in the household, and guests. Stories link to a section, for example
persona-viewer#goals. -->

## Goals

- Open the app they always use, straight away.
- Find a show by typing its name, and learn which app has it, without opening each app in turn.
- Get to something to watch without having to ask the project lead for help.

## Skills and tools

- Uses the remote: the direction pad, select, back, home, and the app buttons (YouTube, Netflix).
- Types a title with the on-screen keyboard.
- Occasionally uses voice search (Gemini) with the remote's voice button, but rarely; it should keep
  working, though nobody would go out of their way for it.
- Can operate a simple, predictable home screen, but does not change settings or configure the TV.
- A guest knows common apps (YouTube, Netflix) by their icons, but has never seen this home screen.

## Context of use

The family room, on the couch, with only the remote. The other adult sometimes watches alone, with
the project lead not there to help; a guest uses the TV with no explanation.

## Frustrations

- Their apps are hard to find among promoted tiles and rows on Google TV's home screen.
- Promoted and sponsored content gets in the way.
- Finding a show means opening app after app to see which has it.
- The home screen keeps changing, so things are not where they were last time.
- When something is not where they expect, they have to call the project lead over.

## Evidence

- All details come from the project lead's answers on 2026-11-09 about the household: who watches
  (the project lead, another adult, guests), what they do (open a favourite app, type a title, ask
  for help), what guests must manage without explanation (find a common app, search), and the four
  frustrations. Nobody else in the household was asked directly. [to validate]
- Replaces the web-app profile's `end-user` starter. Guests were folded in rather than given their own
  persona: they want the same things, with less familiarity.
- Voice search: the project lead uses it occasionally but rarely, and wants the remote's voice
  button to keep working (correction from the project lead, 2026-11-09).
