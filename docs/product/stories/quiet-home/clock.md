# A clock

<!-- docs/product/stories/quiet-home/clock.md. Release: first. -->

As a household viewer, I want to see the time on the home screen, so that I know it without leaving
the TV.

## Acceptance criteria

- The quiet home screen shows the current time, readable from the couch.
- It follows the TV's time zone and its 12- or 24-hour setting, and changes when they change.
