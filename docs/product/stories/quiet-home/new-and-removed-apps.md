# New apps appear, removed apps disappear

<!-- docs/product/stories/quiet-home/new-and-removed-apps.md. Release: first. -->

As a household viewer, I want a newly installed app to show up on its own and an uninstalled one to
go away, so that the home screen matches what is on the TV without anyone fixing it.

## Acceptance criteria

- After an app is installed, it appears at the end of the list the next time the quiet home screen
  is shown, with no one doing anything.
- After an app is uninstalled, it is no longer on the quiet home screen.
- Neither changes where the other apps are.
