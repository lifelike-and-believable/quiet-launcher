# Google TV's own home screen, on request

<!-- docs/product/stories/quiet-home/google-tv.md. Release: first. Added 2026-11-09 at the project
lead's request. -->

As a household viewer, I want to open Google TV's own home screen when I need something only it
has, such as the watchlist or live TV, so that I am never stuck without it.

## Acceptance criteria

- The quiet home screen's top row has a **Google TV** button, between **Voice search** and
  **Settings**.
- Selecting it opens Google TV's own home screen, and it stays: nothing covers it while I use it
  and its pages.
- Pressing Home brings back the quiet home screen, and from then on Google TV's home screen is
  covered again as before.
- If the TV goes to sleep while Google TV's home screen is open, the quiet home screen is what I see
  when it wakes.
