# Undo it

<!-- docs/product/stories/setup/undo.md. Release: first. -->

As the set-up person, I want one written procedure that removes the product and puts the TV back as
it was, so that trying it on the household's TV is safe.

## Acceptance criteria

- Following the written undo procedure from the PC removes the product, in under 15 minutes.
- Afterwards, pressing Home shows Google TV's home screen, and its search, apps, and settings work
  as they did before setup.
- Accessibility settings are back to what they were before setup.
