# Undo it

<!-- docs/product/stories/setup/undo.md. Release: first. -->

As the set-up person, I want one written procedure that removes the product and puts the TV back as
it was, so that trying it on the household's TV is safe.

## Acceptance criteria

- Following the written undo procedure from the PC removes the product in under 15 minutes,
  including when the quiet home screen is not working or setup was left half done.
- Afterwards, pressing Home shows Google TV's home screen, which looks as it did in a screenshot
  taken before setup, apart from the shows in its rows.
- Typed search from Google TV's home screen, opening apps, and the TV's settings work as they did
  before setup.
- The accessibility settings are back to what the setup procedure recorded.
