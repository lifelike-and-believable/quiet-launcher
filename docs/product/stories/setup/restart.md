# It survives a restart

<!-- docs/product/stories/setup/restart.md. Release: first. -->

As the set-up person, I want the quiet home screen to stay the home screen after the TV restarts or
loses power, so that I am not called over after a power cut.

## Acceptance criteria

- After the TV is restarted, or unplugged and plugged back in, pressing Home shows the quiet home
  screen, with no one doing anything at the PC.
- The apps on it are the same as before.
