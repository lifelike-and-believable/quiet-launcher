# Using the quiet home screen

<!-- docs/user/home-screen.md. For the household viewer (persona-viewer): anyone in the family room
with the remote, guests included. Written as if the product exists. Specs cite sections by heading,
for example docs-home-screen#find-which-app-has-a-show. -->

When you press Home on the remote, the TV shows the quiet home screen: your apps and nothing else.
There are no suggested shows, banners, or ads.

Along the top are **Search for a show**, **Voice search**, **Google TV**, **Settings**, and the
time. Below them
are the TV's apps, each with its own picture and its name.

At first your apps will be in different places from where they were on Google TV's home screen:
they are in the order they were installed on the TV, oldest first, until the person who set up the
TV arranges them in your household's order (docs-arranging).

## Open an app

1. Press Home on the remote. The first app is selected (it has a white outline).
2. Move to the app you want with the arrows on the remote.
3. Press the centre button (select).

The app opens. To come back, press Home. Pressing Back until you leave the app should also bring
you back.

If there are more apps than fit on the screen, the next row peeks in at the bottom. Keep pressing
down to reach it. The last app is Google Play Store, for installing new apps.

If an app does not open, the TV shows its own message and you stay on the home screen.

## Find which app has a show

1. Press Home.
2. Press up to reach **Search for a show**, then press select.
3. Type the name of the show with the on-screen keyboard.
4. Press the keyboard's **Search** key.

The screen says it is searching, then Google's search takes over: it dims the screen and shows what
you typed for a few seconds, and then Google's page for the show appears. The page shows where you
can watch it, including any of your apps that has it. Carry on from Google's page to watch.

To search for something else, press Back. You return to the home screen with **Search for a show**
selected and empty.

While typing:

- Back closes the keyboard and keeps what you typed; Back again leaves the search box.
- Pressing **Search** with nothing typed does nothing.
- Home clears what you typed and goes back to the first app.

While the screen says it is searching, only Back (to cancel, keeping what you typed) and Home do
anything.

Sometimes Google answers with a chat reply instead of a page. Press Back and search again with
different words.

## Search by voice

1. Press Home.
2. Move to **Voice search** and press select.
3. Say the name of the show when Google is listening.

You can also hold the voice button on the remote, as before; it works from anywhere.

Press Back to return to the home screen, with **Voice search** selected.

## Open Google TV's own home screen

For things only Google TV's own home screen has, such as the watchlist, Google Play purchases, or
live TV:

1. Press Home.
2. Move to **Google TV** (top right, next to **Settings**) and press select.

Google TV's home screen opens, with its suggestions and banners, and stays while you use it. To
come back to the quiet home screen, press Home. If the TV goes to sleep, it wakes to the quiet home
screen.

## Open the TV's settings

1. Press Home.
2. Move to **Settings** (top right) and press select.

The TV's settings open: network, display, accounts, and so on. Press Back or Home to return.

## The time

The time is at the top right. It follows the TV's own time settings, including whether it uses a
12-hour or 24-hour clock. Like Google TV's clock, the 12-hour clock does not show AM or PM.

## What stays the same, and what changes

- The apps stay where they are. A newly installed app appears at the end; an app that is
  uninstalled disappears. Only the person who set up the TV can move or hide apps.
- If you hold select on an app for a long time, the TV asks whether to arrange the apps. Press
  select on **Cancel**, or press Back: nothing changes.
- Some apps are not shown: the TV's own settings app (use **Settings** instead), Play Games, Google
  TV, other home-screen apps, and any apps the person who set up the TV has hidden. Hidden apps
  still open from search and voice results.
- The remote's YouTube and Netflix buttons, and its voice button, work as they always have.
- Holding down Home does the same as pressing it. Google TV's own long-press Home menu is no longer
  available.
- Back does nothing on the home screen itself, so you cannot leave it by accident.
- Sometimes Google TV's own home screen shows for a moment (less than half a second) before the
  quiet one covers it: for example when you leave an app you opened with the remote's YouTube or
  Netflix button, or when the TV wakes up or starts. That is normal.
- Everyone sees the same home screen, whichever Google TV profile is in use.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Google's search will open in a moment. Back cancels.** for more than a few seconds | Google's search is slow to start | Wait, or press Back to cancel. What you typed is kept |
| **Google's search did not open. Press select to try again.** | Google's search did not start within 10 seconds | Press select on **Search for a show**: what you typed is still there. Then press the keyboard's **Search** key again |
| **No internet connection. Search and voice search need it; your apps still open.** | The TV is not connected to the internet | Your apps still open. If you search anyway, Google shows its own error. The message goes away when the connection is back; if it stays, tell the person who set up the TV |
| A chat reply from Google instead of a page | Google answered in its own way | Press Back and search again with different words |
| **No apps to show. Apps installed on this TV appear here.** | There are no apps on the TV to show | Tell the person who set up the TV |
| Google TV's own home screen, with its rows and banners, and it stays | The quiet home screen has stopped working, for example after a TV update | Everything still works from Google TV's screen. Tell the person who set up the TV |
